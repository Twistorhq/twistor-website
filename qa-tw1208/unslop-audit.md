# Unslop audit, TW-1208 home page (index.astro as built)

Prepared by Twistor Holdings LLC. Mode: polish. One line per sign group,
checked against the built page and the after screenshots at 1440/375.

- Surfaces and color: PASS. No gradients, glows, washes, or glass. One
  neutral paper surface, hairline borders, one accent (burnt orange)
  used for the wordmark dot, status markers, and one headline line.
- Type: PASS with note. No uppercase tracked labels, no italics, no
  negative tracking on small text, money in one format. Headings are
  weight 700 (the sign targets 800/900); 700 is the brand display
  weight on every Twistor page, kept.
- Icons: PASS. No emoji, no icon set; the only marks are the CSS square
  status bullet and the typographic arrow on product links.
- Spacing: PASS. One rhythm (8/12/16/24/28/44/48) across hero, rows,
  and bands; sibling rows share padding and gaps.
- Copy: PASS. No greetings, no exclamation marks, no hype words, no
  trailing periods on buttons, no fake data. Every number is a real,
  dated Twistor fact.
- Components: PASS. No pills, no badges, no card grid, no stat cards.
  Buttons are 8px-radius rectangles; status is plain bold text with a
  square marker; the wordmark is text with an accent dot.

Findings requiring fixes: none.
