CLIMATESHIFT-1 v1

Crop performance under a shifting climate, hemp-first. Thirty trials with full trait panels and compliance-grade provenance, plus a rolling forward-year benchmark.

Get access API endpoints

What is in it

Genotype-by-environment-by-management trial data, starting with hemp. Each trial carries its trait panel: THC, CBD, CBG: plus lab accreditation, certificate-of-analysis hashes, and the management practices behind the numbers. Built to harmonize with G2F and GRIN as the corpus grows.

The trust story

Crop trial data rots when management context goes missing. A yield number without its practices is a rumor. CLIMATESHIFT-1 codes management on every record and quarantines anything incomplete. The rolling forward-year benchmark never lets the test set leak into training: each year predicts the next, in order, the way a breeder actually works.
