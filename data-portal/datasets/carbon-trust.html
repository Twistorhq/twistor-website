CARBON-TRUST v1

Harmonized carbon-sequestration evidence: soil profiles, flux towers, biomass pixels, and a biochar stability annex, with method-code comparability gating and MRV-BLIND, the first falsifiable soil-carbon MRV scoreboard.

Get access API endpoints

What is in it

Five record classes, one harmonized schema. Carbon pools cover soil-organic, aboveground and belowground biomass, biochar-stable, harvested wood, and peat. The biochar stability annex carries feedstock, pyrolysis temperature and residence time, H/C and O/C ratios, aromatic carbon, certification status, and 100-year persistence estimates with the method attached.

The trust story

Soil carbon data has a comparability problem. A Walkley-Black measurement and a dry-combustion measurement are different numbers for the same soil, and most datasets pool them silently. CARBON-TRUST gates on the analytical method code at three layers. Ingest quarantines anything missing or unknown, the gold tier rejects unknown methods outright, and every query surfaces the method on every row with a comparability-group filter. Walkley-Black and dry-combustion never share a group.

MRV-BLIND

The open challenge for soil-carbon MRV methods: sealed plots, predict-then-measure, scored only against dry-combustion-anchored revealed plots. Planted additionality-failure cases keep honest methods honest. Scoring is a pure function (interval score, coverage penalty, reversion bonus), published with the benchmark.
