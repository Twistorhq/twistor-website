S2S-TRUST v1

Subseasonal prediction, weeks 2 to 6, the predictability desert, with truth-lineage schema, a quarantine for incomplete provenance, and a sealed-window benchmark harness.

Get access API endpoints

What is in it

Forecast and verification data across the subseasonal range, built on ERA5 reanalysis, ECMWF reforecasts, and NOAA GEFS. Every record carries its truth source: the verification data a forecast is scored against is named, versioned, and traceable, so you always know what "correct" meant.

The trust story

Subseasonal skill claims are easy to inflate: pick the right truth dataset and any model looks good. S2S-TRUST labels the truth lineage on every record and quarantines anything with incomplete provenance before it can reach the gold tier. The sealed-window benchmark harness scores predictions against held-out windows on a fixed schedule: no peeking, no re-rolling.
